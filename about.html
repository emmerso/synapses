About SDA Consultancy

Making data proficiency practical, accessible, and useful.

Synapse Data Analytics Consultancy, known simply as SDA Consultancy, is a registered Zimbabwean research and educational firm blending world-class statistical training and digital skills with data analytics consultancy.

World-class skills, grounded in local impact.
