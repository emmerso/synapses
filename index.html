FAQs

Questions, answered clearly.

Who can attend SDA Consultancy training?

Our programmes can support organisations, professionals, graduates, students, and individuals who want practical data and digital skills.

Do you offer online and physical sessions?

Yes. SDA Consultancy can provide physical or online delivery depending on the programme, cohort, and client requirements.

Which tools do you teach?

Our training includes Advanced Excel with AI, Power BI, Python, R, STATA, SPSS, and AI or Machine Learning with Python.

Can Consultancy support Monitoring and Evaluation?

Yes. We design M&E frameworks, indicators, tools, reporting systems, and analysis approaches that help organisations measure impact.

How do I enquire about POTRAZ training?

Use the contact form or WhatsApp SDA Consultancy with your organisation, role, and preferred training needs. We will guide you through the next step.
